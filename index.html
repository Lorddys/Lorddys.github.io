Good to know

  • Your data stays on your phone. BillsAtHome works 100% on your device: there is no account, no cloud and no server. We cannot see, recover or restore your contracts and payments.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data. Because everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling BillsAtHome removes all of its data with it.
  • Reminders are local. Payment reminders are notifications created on your own device. If they stop appearing, check that notifications for BillsAtHome are allowed in your phone's settings.
  • It works offline. No connection is needed for any feature.
